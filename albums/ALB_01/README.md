# Normans in the South

![Normans in the South](assets/ALB001_.png)

**Normans in the South** is the first album in a medieval rock/metal saga about the Norman arrival in southern Italy. It follows the first legendary encounters, mercenary service, defeat, survival, and the founding of Aversa — the first lasting Norman foothold in Italy.

The album begins with pilgrims and adventurers drawn into the conflicts of Lombards, Byzantines, Saracens, princes, and emperors. By the end, the Normans are no longer passing warriors: they have learned the southern game, taken land, and begun to shape a new age.
